# Mission Reflection

This mission helped me understand cloud computing better. Before doing this activity, I thought cloud computing was mainly about online storage and virtual servers. After learning about AWS, Azure, and GCP, I realized that cloud platforms provide many different services for businesses and organizations.

The cloud platform that interested me the most was **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)**. I liked its services for Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, data processing, and Kubernetes. I also learned that AWS, Azure, and GCP have similar services, including virtual machines, storage, networking, databases, and user access management.

I learned that choosing a cloud platform depends on what a company or organization needs. Things such as cost, security, performance, scalability, and existing technology should be considered. For example, a university that already uses Microsoft products may benefit from Azure. An AI company may prefer GCP because of its AI and Machine Learning services. AWS can be useful for companies that need a wide variety of cloud services.

Being a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that I should understand the client's needs before recommending a cloud platform. I learned that there is no single cloud platform that is perfect for every organization.

This mission also helped me improve my GitHub portfolio. I added my research about AWS, Azure, and GCP, cloud service comparisons, client recommendations, the decision matrix, and my KillerCoda Linux investigation.

Overall, this activity gave me a better understanding of cloud computing. It also helped me improve how I organize and present my work in a clear and professional way.
